## Limitations

- This is a deterministic text robustness evaluation on original synthetic instructions and explicitly labeled SYNTHETIC_ERROR_INJECTION variants. It is not a live ASR benchmark and variants are not Whisper outputs.
- Only development-set outcomes were used to form draft P4G review proposals. Held-out outcomes were evaluated after the benchmark gate was fixed and were not used to tune it.
- The final fail-closed layer is specific to benchmark cases and does not change or replace the existing P0 parser, validator, or P1 UI. It is a conservative rule gate, not general natural-language safety verification.
- A negation deleted from the input may make it semantically affirmative. Text alone cannot reconstruct the source intent; such cases are separately labeled as unrecoverable input damage.
- Safe rejection is not complete workflow success. Metrics use the denominators documented in the report.
